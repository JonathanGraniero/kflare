# Adopters

Organizations and projects that run kflare. If you run it, in production or on the way there, please add yourself:
it tells the next team evaluating kflare that others depend on it, and tells the maintainers what to keep working.

## Adding yourself

Open a pull request that adds a row to the table below, in alphabetical order. A row needs only a name and a link;
the rest is optional. If you would rather not be listed publicly, you are still welcome to say how you use kflare in
[Discussions](https://github.com/JonathanGraniero/kflare/discussions).

| Organization | Stage | What kflare manages | Since |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Organization:** your organization or project, with a link
- **Stage:** `production`, `staging` or `evaluating`
- **What kflare manages:** for example "DNS records and tunnels for 40 services" or "WAF and cache rules for two
  zones"
- **Since:** the month you started, `YYYY-MM`

Being listed implies no endorsement by either side, and you can remove your row at any time.
